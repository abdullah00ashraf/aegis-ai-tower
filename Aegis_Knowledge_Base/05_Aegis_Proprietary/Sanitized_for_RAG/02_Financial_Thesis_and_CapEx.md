## 1.0 Executive Framing: This Is a Software Investment

Before engaging with the capital structure below, the following framing is mandatory context for all prospective investors:

**You are not funding a building. You are funding Aegis OS.**

The 26-floor tower in Maharashtra is the hardware validation substrate for a proprietary, hardware-agnostic neural operating system. The physical CapEx is a one-time cost of instantiating the proof-of-concept. The licensable Aegis OS — the Bi-LSTM inference kernel, the PINN Safety Layer, the Dispatch Protocol specification — is the commercial asset.

The financial model below demonstrates that the *physical building alone*, independent of any software licensing revenue, achieves positive cash-flow through operational autonomy by **Month 48**. The software licensing upside is not included in this base-case model. It represents pure upside.

## 4.0 The ROI Model: Month 48 Cross-Over

### 4.1 Mathematical Proof of ROI Cross-Over

The ROI cross-over event is defined as the exact month at which **cumulative OpEx and Energy savings equal the initial aggregate DeepTech CapEx outlay**.

**Given:**
- Aggregate DeepTech CapEx (C₀): ₹ 27.62 Crore
- Annual Savings Yield (S_annual): ₹ 6.90 Crore
- Monthly Savings Yield (S_monthly): ₹ 0.575 Crore

**Cross-Over Month Calculation:**

```
Cross-Over Month (T*) = C₀ / S_monthly
T* = ₹ 27.62 Crore / ₹ 0.575 Crore per month
T* = 48.03 months
```

**At exactly Month 48, every subsequent month of building operation generates pure operational margin.** This margin compounds for the 40+ year operational lifecycle of the structure.

### 4.2 Investor Return Framework

| Phase | Timeframe | Economic State |
|---|---|---|
| **Phase 0: Construction & Fit-Out** | Month 0–24 | CapEx deployment; no revenue |
| **Phase 1: Commissioning & Stabilization** | Month 25–36 | Revenue begins; OpEx = ₹ 0; CapEx amortizing |
| **Phase 2: ROI Acceleration** | Month 37–47 | Cumulative savings approaching CapEx parity |
| **Phase 3: Cross-Over Event** | **Month 48** | **Cumulative savings = DeepTech CapEx** |
| **Phase 4: Pure Profit Horizon** | Month 49 – Year 40+ | 100% of savings = unmitigated operating profit |

### 4.3 The Excluded Upside: Software Licensing

The above model deliberately excludes all software licensing revenue — the Aegis OS SaaS tier. At the point of flagship validation (Month 36), the Aegis OS kernel enters commercial licensing negotiation with legacy real estate developers.

**If Aegis OS is licensed at ₹ 2.5 Crore per deployment** to 50 partner developers across 5 years, the licensing revenue alone exceeds the aggregate tower CapEx by a factor of 3.6x.

This is the asymmetric upside profile of an infrastructure investment that is simultaneously a software company.
